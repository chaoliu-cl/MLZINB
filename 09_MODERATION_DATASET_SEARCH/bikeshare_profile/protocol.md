# Revised daily-profile model: before validation interaction results

The original midday-specific discovery result had a strong count interaction (LR 620.7) but essentially unidentified inflation coefficients (SE approximately 10,000 for the focal product). That is not an acceptable regular four-model demonstration. All original fits are retained in ../bikeshare/outputs. The initial 2012 additive fit was stopped; no 2012 interaction-model results have been inspected. This amendment is prompted by the discovery identification check, not by the validation p-value.

Revised question: Does working-day context modify the within-day clock-time profile of casual rentals? This is a social-science, contextual human-mobility example: hours nested in days, with workingday constant by day.

Primary sample: days containing exactly 24 distinct released clock-hour records. These 655 days provide 15,720 records, balanced coverage and exactly zero day-mean sine/cosine predictors. Do not fill absent records as zeros. Days with incomplete coverage remain in a planned sensitivity analysis. No additional outcome-based deletion is permitted.

Discovery: complete days in 2011. Validation: complete days in 2012. Full sample: secondary estimation after both. Main decision: require discovery and validation joint interaction tests at .05 and inspect group-aware/HAC inference. For transparency report the two candidate datasets and three model questions attempted, including the original nonregular midpoint gate; use a conservative three-screen Bonferroni comparison (.05/3) for the selected final result. This is an exploratory model-development search with temporal validation in one system, not a preregistered or independent-city replication.

Clock time is represented by the first two sine/cosine harmonics in the count component (4 terms), and the first harmonic in the inflation component (2 terms). These are within-day predictors with known mean zero on complete days. There is no separately estimated between-day mean for a mathematically constant quantity. Context is workingday (0/1). The contextual products are those 4 terms in the count component and 2 terms in inflation. Four matched NB2 ML-ZINBs add none, count products only, inflation products only, or both. The primary joint comparison has 6 df, not 2. Conditional component additions have 4 count df or 2 inflation df.

Count controls: month indicators, context, released normalized temperature and its square, humidity, wind speed, badweather (weather codes 3/4 pooled), and day random intercept. Inflation controls: context, normalized temperature, humidity and badweather. The first hour harmonic is in every inflation model. These equations are fixed before fitting the revised models; validation formulas and sample rules will match discovery. No arbitrary flat coefficient is fixed in this analysis.

Full-sample pooling amendment before fitting validation or full models: include a 2012 indicator in both components of the pooled full-sample models, to account for system growth. It is constant within each separate-year analysis and therefore is omitted there. All four models within a sample share the same year treatment.

Fit a same-count-specification NB2 comparator. Check ordinary convergence, Hessians, gradients, large coefficients/SEs, and optimizer reproducibility. A negligible random-intercept variance is reported as a boundary; a nonregular comparison must be qualified. Do not claim zeros prove a latent non-rental state. Seasonal growth and absent inventory exposure remain limitations.

Primary uncertainty uses day marginal-score sandwich covariance, including all nuisance parameters, and a calendar-time Bartlett HAC covariance at 14-day lags. Absent days occupy their calendar positions, rather than being made adjacent. Lag 7/28 sensitivities. Reconstruct per-day Laplace likelihood and scores independently against glmmTMB before accepting inference. Temporal validation in the same city is not an independent-system replication.

Report the 4-df count product test, 2-df inflation product test and 6-df joint test, estimates/covariances and hourly component predictions. Visualize mu, pi, E(Y), total-zero probability and workingday minus nonworkingday contrasts; overlay the additive model. All predictions must update the harmonics consistently with clock hour and state the random-effect target. Average mixtures recordwise over an equal-day empirical reference distribution. Do not multiply separately averaged mu and 1-pi.

Diagnostics: 500 unconditional simulations; count, zero and positive-count calibration by time band/day type; residual uniformity/dispersion/zeros at observation and day levels; serial residual display. Sensitivities: third count harmonic and interactions; second inflation harmonic and interactions; independent day random slope(s); inflation day random intercept; full count hour-factor by context model (a global daily-profile test with corresponding df rather than relabeled harmonic coefficients); temperature-by-context adjustment; all released days; BFGS optimizer. Retain numerical failures.

Influence: first-order deletion for every primary day, followed by exact reoptimized deletions for the five largest approximate influences and five fixed evenly spaced dates. Report counts, methods, failures and whether approximation agrees. A verified sandwich/HAC is the grouping-aware uncertainty method; do not claim a full refit bootstrap was done.

The final result may establish a statistical contextual association in clock-time profiles. It cannot establish a causal effect of day type, a biological structural-zero class, a neighborhood mechanism, or transportability to other systems. Remaining lack of fit must be reported with its magnitude. If reasonable richer models remove the signal or fail identification, qualify accordingly.
